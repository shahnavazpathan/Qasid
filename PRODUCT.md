# Product Truth

## Core Value
Qasid enables retail investors to track Indian IPOs and automate the allotment checking process across 10 different registrars, ensuring they never miss an allotment result or key subscription data.

## Key Features
- **Allotment Checker**: Automated, bulk PAN checking across 10 registrars.
- **IPO Dashboard**: Live GMP trends, category-wise subscription data, and IPO status tracking.
- **IPO Planner**: Calendar-based planning tool with budget optimization.
- **Alerts**: Push notifications for new allotments and IPO status changes.

## Constraints & Realities
- **Mobile-first**: The user base is heavily mobile-focused, prioritizing scanability and rapid interaction.
- **Private API**: No public API exists. Qasid proxies registrar and exchange data.
- **Performance**: High reliability needed during allotment publishing windows.
- **Visuals**: Currently functional but needing a professional, modern redesign.

## Stakeholder/User Goal
To empower individual investors to participate efficiently and effectively in the Indian IPO market without the manual hassle of registrar site navigation.
