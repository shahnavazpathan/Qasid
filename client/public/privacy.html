Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how Qasid collects and uses your information when you use our IPO tracking service.

Data We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we collect your email address, name, and profile picture to identify you and manage your saved PANs.
  • User Data: We store the PANs you explicitly choose to save to track your IPO allotment status.

How We Use Your Data

  • We use your Google identity to securely link your saved PANs to your account.
  • We use your PANs solely to provide you with allotment status updates.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Data Deletion

You can remove your saved PANs at any time from your account settings. If you wish to delete your entire account and associated data, please contact us at sk7611065@gmail.com.
